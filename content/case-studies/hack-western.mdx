# Overview
## What they didn't know was this.

Hack Western is one of Canada's largest student hackathons, bringing together over 2,000 hackers for a weekend of building, learning, and connecting.

Each year, thousands of students apply through an online portal. Our challenge: make that experience feel as exciting as the event itself.

# Challenge

The existing application experience was fragmented and confusing. Applicants dropped off at high rates mid-flow, and many reached out to organizers with basic navigation questions.

We needed to reduce friction, set expectations clearly, and inject some of Hack Western's personality into an otherwise dry process.

# The theme

This year's theme was adventure and exploration — a nod to pushing boundaries and charting new territory. Every design decision, from color to copy, was filtered through this lens.

# App. portal ideation

Early ideation focused on reducing cognitive load across the multi-step form. We mapped the full application journey and identified the top three drop-off points.

From there, we explored ways to make each step feel like progress — using a visual stepper, celebratory micro-moments, and clear copy that explained exactly what we'd do with each piece of information.

# Designing for mobile

Over 70% of applicants start on mobile. We locked in the mobile experience first, then adapted to desktop — not the other way around.

Key decisions: a single-column form layout, large tap targets, and a persistent progress indicator that kept applicants oriented.

# The stickerbook

A standout delight feature was a digital sticker collection. Applicants earned stickers by completing milestones — submitting their application, confirming attendance, joining the Discord.

It gave applicants a reason to come back to the portal and built anticipation in the weeks before the event.

# Final product

The redesigned portal shipped to 2,000+ hackers. Drop-off rate in the first two steps dropped by 40%, and organizer support tickets related to navigation fell by over half.
